FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a website?

Delivery starts from 2 business days for our Starter package, up to 7 days for Premium. Enterprise projects are scoped individually. You receive a confirmed timeline at the start of every project.

What's included in the package price?

Every package includes design, development, mobile responsiveness, SEO setup, a contact form, and fast customer support. No hidden fees. The price you see is the price you pay, tax included.

Can I request changes after delivery?

Yes. Each package includes a set number of revision rounds. After delivery, ongoing changes can be handled through our support plans or quoted separately.

Will my website work on mobile and tablet?

Every site we build is fully responsive, tested on phones, tablets, and desktops across Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge.

Do you work with clients outside Belgium?

Absolutely. We work with clients worldwide. All project communication is handled remotely via WhatsApp, email, and video calls.

Can I manage my own content after launch?

We build in CMS options for clients who want self-editing capability. If you'd prefer us to handle updates, our support plans cover that too.

How much does a website cost in Dubai?

Our packages start from €200. Dubai and international clients receive the same pricing with no extra fees. Message us on WhatsApp for a custom quote tailored to your business.

Do you build websites in Arabic?

Yes. We specialise in bilingual and Arabic websites with full right-to-left layout. We serve clients in Dubai, Saudi Arabia, Jordan, and across the Arab world.

Can you build a website for a restaurant, clinic, or garage?

Absolutely. We build websites for all types of businesses - restaurants with online booking, medical clinics, car garages, lawyers, salons, real estate agencies, e-commerce shops, and more. Every site is custom-built for your industry.

Do you build websites in the Netherlands and Germany?

Yes. We have clients across the Netherlands, Belgium, and Germany. We build in Dutch and German and our websites are optimised for local Google search so your business gets found in your region.

Will my website rank on Google?

Every website we build includes SEO setup - fast loading, mobile optimisation, clean code, meta tags, and schema markup. You start on the right foundation from day one.

How do I get a free quote?

Message us on WhatsApp or email info@webmaxpro.com. Tell us your business type and what you need, and we will send a custom quote within 24 hours.
