Our Story

WebMaxPro was founded on a simple frustration: too many businesses were paying too much for websites that were too slow, too generic, and too forgettable. The web design market was — and still is — full of agencies charging premium rates for WordPress templates dressed up as custom work, and freelancers on platforms like Fiverr delivering work that does more harm than good to the businesses it was supposed to help.

We started WebMaxPro to be different. Not just different in what we promise, but in how we actually work. Every website we build is written by hand — clean HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, without a bloated theme or page builder in sight. The result is sites that load in under a second, score 90+ on Google PageSpeed, and look nothing like anyone else's.

We serve businesses across Belgium, the Netherlands, Germany, France, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Jordan, Egypt, Lebanon, and beyond. We work in English, Dutch, French, and German, and we build websites in virtually any language — including Arabic with full right-to-left support. Our clients range from solo consultants and local restaurants to established professional service firms and regional e-commerce brands.

Our Values

Craft Over Speed

We write every line of code with intention. No copy-paste templates, no drag-and-drop builders. The craft shows in the details — in the load time, the spacing, the scroll behaviour, the readability on every device.

Radical Transparency

Our pricing is public. Our process is clear. We tell you exactly what we're building, when you'll receive it, and what it will cost. No scope creep surprises. No disappearing mid-project.

Results, Not Vanity

Beautiful is necessary but not sufficient. Every design decision we make is tested against a simple question: does this help the visitor take the action the client needs? If not, it doesn't belong on the page.

Long-Term Thinking

We build websites you'll own outright, on clean code any developer can maintain. No platform lock-in, no subscription dependency, no forced upgrades. Your website should be an asset, not a liability.

How We Work

Our process is designed to be efficient and collaborative without being burdensome for busy business owners. It starts with a discovery conversation — we learn about your business, your audience, your goals, and what you need the website to achieve. We then produce a design mockup for your review before writing a single line of code.

Once you approve the direction, we build. Most standard projects are complete within 7 business days from the moment we have all your content. We provide a staging link for your review, incorporate your feedback, and then launch on your hosting on a date that suits you. Post-launch, we provide a handover walkthrough and remain available for questions and updates.

We're honest about what we don't do: we don't do brand strategy, we don't run ad campaigns, and we don't offer social media management. We do one thing — build excellent websites — and we do it better than almost anyone at our price point. That focus is what keeps our quality consistently high and our delivery consistently fast.

Our Commitment to You

When you work with WebMaxPro, you get a direct line to the people actually building your site. No junior staff handoffs, no account managers acting as middlemen. You get clear communication, honest timelines, and a finished product that you're proud to share. If something isn't right, we fix it. Our goal isn't just a satisfied client at delivery — it's a client who refers their friends two years later because their website still delivers results.
